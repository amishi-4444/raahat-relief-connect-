Simple by design

Help should be easy to find.

One straightforward flow for people who need help, volunteers who can offer it, and coordinators who keep everyone informed.

01

Tell us what’s happening

Choose the kind of support, urgency, and general area. Share only what is needed to coordinate safely.

02

Find a nearby helper

Match requests with verified volunteers based on skills, availability, distance, and safety requirements.

03

Coordinate and follow up

In a production system, a coordinator can confirm the match, share status updates, and close the request.
